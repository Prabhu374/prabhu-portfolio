  1. 1

    The order arrives

    The hospital's HIS/EMR sends a radiology order over HL7/FHIR. My order-processing APIs and integration services bring it into the RIS.

  2. 2

    It joins the worklist

    Worklist management APIs place the exam on the radiology worklist and keep it current.

  3. 3

    Images are stored

    The study is stored in PACS, and real-time data exchange keeps RIS and PACS in step.

  4. 4

    The report goes back

    Report generation APIs produce the radiology report, which flows back to the hospital's EMR.
